# Mi Ciudadania - MVP validation pitch

## 1. Thesis
Mi Ciudadania helps Spanish-speaking lawful permanent residents in the United States prepare for naturalization without turning a routine case into an expensive legal engagement.

The wedge is deliberately narrow: Spanish-speaking green card holders who may qualify through residence and need clarity, structure, study support, and interview practice.

## 2. Problem
Naturalization is not one problem. It is four repeated anxieties:

- Am I eligible now, or am I about to waste the filing fee?
- What do I need to prepare before I submit Form N-400?
- How do I study the official civics material in a way I can remember?
- What will the interview feel like?

For routine cases, many applicants do not need full legal representation. They need a practical, bilingual guide that tells them when to proceed, when to stop, and when to talk to a lawyer.

## 3. Market signal
CRS, using DHS estimates, reported about 9.04 million lawful permanent residents potentially eligible to naturalize in 2023. The same CRS table shows Mexico alone at about 2.39 million potentially eligible LPRs, with additional Spanish-speaking origin countries such as Cuba, the Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Colombia, Guatemala, and Ecuador forming a large initial wedge.

Source: https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R43366

## 4. Audience
Initial customer:

- Spanish-speaking permanent resident in the U.S.
- Has had a green card for about 5 years, or 3 years while married to a U.S. citizen
- Is not trying to solve a complex immigration dispute
- Wants to avoid unnecessary attorney cost but still wants structure and confidence

Secondary customer:

- Immigration attorneys and nonprofits that want a low-friction prep tool for routine education, lead qualification, and civics practice without replacing legal counsel.

## 5. Product
Mi Ciudadania is a simple web app that works on phone and computer:

- Eligibility scan for common N-400 readiness factors
- Route N-400 checklist and progress tracker
- Civics study cards for the current naturalization test
- Interview simulation and confidence practice
- Legal disclaimer and stop-sign prompts for cases needing attorney review

## 6. Positioning
Not a law firm. Not government. Not a form-filling black box.

Mi Ciudadania is a practical preparation layer: the guide before the decision, the checklist before the form, the study tool before the interview, and the warning system before a risky filing.

## 7. Money flow
MVP revenue path:

- Free: public landing, legal disclaimer, sample eligibility scan, sample civics cards
- Paid user plan: $29-$49 one-time access for 90 days of guided preparation
- Add-on: $9-$15 interview practice pack
- Attorney channel: $79-$149 monthly co-branded study portal or practice-room license

Money movement:

1. User pays Mi Ciudadania directly for education and workflow software.
2. Mi Ciudadania delivers self-guided preparation, not legal advice.
3. If a case is flagged as complex, the app recommends attorney review.
4. Attorneys can pay for software access or sponsorship. Avoid fee-splitting or per-case legal referral fees unless reviewed by ethics counsel.

## 8. Why now
The naturalization pathway is digitizing, but preparation remains fragmented. USCIS/USAGov provide official rules and study material, while users still need a guided workflow in Spanish that connects eligibility, process control, studying, and interview practice.

USAGov notes basic naturalization requirements and that applicants filing Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025 must take the 2025 civics test with 128 questions.

Source: https://www.usa.gov/es/ciudadania-estados-unidos-naturalizacion/

## 9. Competitive frame
Most alternatives sit in different jobs:

- USCIS/USAGov: official information, not guided product experience
- Law firms: necessary for complex cases, high-trust but higher cost
- Nonprofits: trusted and low-cost, but capacity-limited
- Study apps: civics practice, not process control
- Form services: filing workflow, not readiness and confidence

Mi Ciudadania starts where those experiences leave a gap.

## 10. Go-to-market
Start with one dense wedge:

- Spanish-language landing page
- Local ads in 2-3 metro areas with high Spanish-speaking LPR populations
- WhatsApp share loops and community workshops
- Partnerships with Spanish-speaking accountants, libraries, churches, adult English programs, and immigration attorneys
- Attorney channel as ally: “send routine prep here; keep complex legal work in the firm”

## 11. MVP validation metrics
Do not raise capital yet. Prove willingness to pay.

- Landing conversion to eligibility scan: 18%-25%
- Scan completion: 55%-70%
- Paid conversion from completed scan: 4%-8%
- Cost per paid user target: under $18 in local paid channels
- Week-one activation: complete one roadmap task and 20 civics cards
- Referral signal: 15%+ users share with family or WhatsApp group

## 12. Contrarian insight
The naturalization market is often treated as legal services or static content. The underbuilt layer is procedural confidence: a trustworthy, bilingual operating system for applicants whose cases are routine but whose anxiety is real.

The first monopoly is not “immigration.” It is Spanish-speaking N-400 preparation for routine green-card-based naturalization, done well enough that users and attorneys both prefer it.
