# Mi Ciudadania - Launch campaign and identity system

## Brand idea
Citizenship preparation in your hand.

Mi Ciudadania gives Spanish-speaking permanent residents a clear, practical path to check readiness, organize the N-400 journey, study civics questions, and practice the interview.

## Brand position
Educational software for routine naturalization preparation. It complements legal help and routes complex cases to attorneys.

## Taglines
- Tu camino a la ciudadania, paso a paso.
- Preparacion clara para tu N-400.
- Estudia, organiza y practica con calma.
- Una guia practica. No un reemplazo legal.

## Visual identity
Palette:

- Civic blue: #1f6f8b
- Paper: #fbf7ef
- Ink: #152336
- Signal red: #cf4e55
- Oath gold: #c9933e
- Progress green: #4b9f68

Typography:

- Headline: confident serif for life-event gravity.
- Interface/body: clean sans-serif for clarity and accessibility.

Logo direction:

- A simple civic doorway/checkmark mark.
- Avoid eagles, official seals, government-style crests, or USCIS-like symbols.
- The mark should feel like a guide, not an authority.

## Campaign promise
For many routine green-card-based naturalization applicants, the hardest part is not needing a lawyer. It is knowing what to do, when to pause, and how to prepare.

## Launch audience
Primary:

- Spanish-speaking LPRs in the U.S.
- Age 25-60
- Mobile-first, often family-supported
- Ready or nearly ready to apply

Secondary:

- Immigration attorneys
- Nonprofits
- Libraries and adult education centers
- Community organizations

## MVP launch sequence
Week 1:

- Publish landing page.
- Run 3 message tests: cost clarity, interview confidence, N-400 checklist.
- Recruit 5 attorney/community advisors.

Weeks 2-3:

- Launch $29-$49 paid beta.
- Track conversion and support tickets.
- Interview first 25 users.

Weeks 4-6:

- Add most-requested workflow fixes.
- Test attorney co-branded portal.
- Publish transparent update notes.

Decision gate:

- Continue only if users pay and complete core actions.
- Do not raise capital until there is paid retention and repeatable acquisition.
